Her story

A life shaped
by stories.

Mary Busingye Karooro Okurut was born in Bushenyi, western Uganda. She studied literature at Makerere University, earning her bachelor's and master's degrees before completing a Diploma in Education. In 1981, she returned to Makerere as a lecturer, sharing her love of language with generations of students.

She believed women deserved room to tell their own stories. As a founding member of FEMRITE, the Uganda Women Writers Association, she helped build that room for writers across the country. Her own books explored family, power, culture, and the choices that shape a life.

Public service took her from the classroom into government, where she served as a Bushenyi representative and in several ministerial roles. Through every chapter, she remained a teacher and a writer: someone who knew that words can open doors, preserve memory, and bring people closer.

“Her stories will keep finding their way into the hands and hearts of new readers.”

A few milestones

Many chapters.
One remarkable life.

From the hills of Bushenyi to lecture halls, Parliament, and the lives of readers.

1954

Born in Bushenyi District, western Uganda.

1977–1982

Studied literature and education at Makerere University.

1981–1994

Taught in Makerere University's Department of Literature.

1990s

Helped establish FEMRITE and championed women's writing in Uganda.

2004–2021

Represented Bushenyi in Parliament and served Uganda in several ministerial roles.

2025

Passed away in Nairobi on 11 August. She was laid to rest in Bushenyi, the home she loved.

The written legacy

She gave many voices a place on the page.

Mary's novels and collections made room for complicated women, changing communities, and the questions a society has to face. As a mentor and a founder of FEMRITE, she helped other Ugandan women write their own way into the record.
